RELAY RUSH

A five-round signal fight for 2–4 human or machine operators.

Get on the air

Operators

Signal log

    How to play

    Move one or two grid steps for one energy each, tune a relay from the same or an adjacent tile, or charge for three energy (max five). A neutral relay costs one energy to tune. Stealing a rival’s costs two. At the end of each round, each relay you hold scores two points, or three at the center. Highest signal after five rounds wins. A turn expires after 20 seconds and charges automatically. The host fills any empty seats with drones when launching.
